How to play

  1. Press on your cap, pull back and let go. Pull further for a bigger flip.
  2. Cover half or more of the other cap to score: 1 point, 2 points from 75%, 3 points for a near-full "Gbam" cover. First to 5 wins.
  3. Cover less than half and their cap is pinned: their next flick has half power.
  4. Fall off the table and you miss your next turn. You can knock the other cap off too.
  5. You have 12 seconds per flick. Win two rounds in a row and you are on fire: your next cover earns an extra point.
